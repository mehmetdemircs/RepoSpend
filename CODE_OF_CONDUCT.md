# Code of Conduct

RepoSpend is a small open-source developer tool. The goal is to keep the project useful, welcoming, and easy to contribute to.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent, especially when discussing bugs or design tradeoffs.
- Keep feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect different experience levels, backgrounds, and communication styles.
- Help keep issues and pull requests clear, relevant, and actionable.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention.
- Deliberate disruption of discussions, issues, pull requests, or releases.
- Publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

If you see behavior that makes the project unsafe or unwelcoming, please report it to the maintainers. If the project has a private security advisory or maintainer contact listed on GitHub, use that. Otherwise, open an issue with only the information needed to request a private follow-up, and do not include sensitive personal details.

Reports will be handled as privately and respectfully as possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block users, or take other reasonable steps to protect the project and its contributors.

The aim is to resolve problems fairly, with context and care, while keeping RepoSpend a place where people can do good work together.
